Who I Am

The Analyst Behind the Numbers

I'm Shreyas Patel, a results-driven professional transitioning into data analytics after 4+ years building operational expertise across technical support, store management, and financial operations. My background in Computer Engineering gives me a strong foundation and now I'm channeling it into data.

At Concentrix, I manage 600+ cases monthly and every case is a data point. I designed troubleshooting frameworks that cut repeat escalations by 25 to 30%, built resolution templates that improved First Contact Resolution, and maintained 100% accuracy in sensitive financial transaction processing. That's not just support work, that's operational analytics in practice.

My career move into data analysis isn't a leap in the dark. It's a natural progression. I've been living data all along: tracking KPIs, spotting fraud patterns, analyzing CSAT trends, and mentoring teams using insight-driven feedback loops.

Why Data Analysis? I realized that my strongest moments at work weren't solving individual tickets. They were when I spotted a pattern across hundreds of them and changed the process. That's where data lives, and that's where I want to be.
